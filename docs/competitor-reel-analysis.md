## What the winners share

1. **Hook is a claim plus a number, in the first 4 seconds.** "Old Android phone", "24-7", "didn't cost me a dime". The best two reels (63k to 65k likes) use the identical hook line. Reusing a proven hook beats inventing a new one.
2. **One idea per reel.** Phone becomes server, free, no port forwarding. The reel that tried to compare three products (3k likes) did worst.
3. **Minecraft-animation character instead of a face.** A blocky avatar walks through a shader-lit Minecraft world while narration plays. The app UI is overlaid as a phone-shaped screen recording, 2 to 4 seconds per screen.
4. **Word-by-word, bold, centered captions** on every reel, one word at a time, large, white with dark outline. Placed in the lower-middle of the frame.
5. **Fast pace.** Roughly one visual change every 2 to 3 seconds, plus small pop-ups (price tag floating above a phone, Wi-Fi bars, a "$$$" icon, a question-mark burst).
6. **Same three-beat script** in almost every reel:
   - Hook: the pain or the surprising claim.
   - Proof: tap Create Server, get a public address, friends join, no port forwarding.
   - Payoff and call to action: "your old phone might be the best server you already own", then Google Play logo and "Download on Google Play Store".
7. **The ask is soft and specific**: a question to drive comments ("What phone are you using?"), then store badge, plus "Link in bio".
8. **Honest limits build trust**: "still in alpha", "public address works with Java for now". This makes the big claims believable.
9. **Price-ladder test** (cheap, mid, flagship phone) gives a built-in story arc: struggle, sweet spot, "it flies". It works as a series; reuse it with different phones.
10. **Descriptions** repeat the hook, then a link-in-bio line, then 4 to 6 hashtags (#minecraft #minecraftserver #android #mobilegaming).

## Production checklist

- 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 to 45 seconds for story reels, 15 seconds for meme loop.
- Word-by-word captions, large, bold, outlined.
- Cut or change visual every 2 to 3 seconds.
- Phone-shaped app screen recordings, 2 to 4 seconds each.
- Floating price tags and icons for emphasis.
- End card: PocketHost logo, Google Play badge, "Link in bio".
- Use fresh sections of the parkour background footage for each reel (never repeat a stretch).

## Caution

Do not copy their footage, avatar, or exact wording beyond the generic hook structure. Reuse the format and the structure, not their assets.
